# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective clients, collaborators, and hiring teams evaluating Felix Joseph Castañeda's ability to understand operational problems and deliver complete web and AI systems.

## Product Purpose

Present Felix as a Full-Stack Web and AI Developer focused on agentic AI and automation. The portfolio should make his approach understandable, give visitors confidence in the quality of his work, and create a direct path to a conversation.

## Positioning

Felix works from the real operation outward: he studies repetitive work and handoffs, builds the full product and automation around them, and improves the system based on whether it creates meaningful value.

## Operating Context

Visitors move through a single portfolio narrative: Hero → About → Skills → Projects → Testimonial → Experience → Contact. Projects, employment details, testimonial attribution, and public contact details remain replaceable until Felix supplies verified information.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js App Router, TypeScript, React, Motion, and GSAP.
- Responsive light and dark themes.
- Smooth interaction with a complete reduced-motion experience.
- Public contact channels are Facebook, email, and phone only.
- Do not add GitHub, LinkedIn, fabricated employers, or unverified outcome claims.

## Brand Commitments

- Public identity: Felix Joseph Castañeda — Full-Stack Web & AI Developer.
- Voice: practical, direct, curious, and ownership-oriented.
- Monochrome visual foundation with tightly controlled spectral light.
- Nohemi is the display typeface; supporting text must remain highly readable.
- References establish ambition and interaction ideas, never a layout to copy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Approved biography and positioning copy supplied in the project brief.
- Draft testimonial without approved attribution.
- Three explicitly labeled placeholder project concepts and target outcomes.
- No approved project screenshots, employer history, contact values, or testimonial attribution yet.

## Product Principles

- Show the mechanism, not only the claim.
- Make complex systems feel clear and purposeful.
- Treat motion as hierarchy and explanation.
- Preserve trust by labeling every placeholder honestly.
- Give every viewport an intentional, finished composition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-complete controls, visible focus, legible contrast, comfortable touch targets, semantic landmarks, and an equivalent `prefers-reduced-motion` experience are required.
